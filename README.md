## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am an **Exploration Geologist and Geoscience Researcher** specializing in **mineral exploration, remote sensing, GIS, critical minerals, rare earth elements (REEs), and mineral prospectivity mapping**.

My work integrates:

- 🛰️ **Multi-sensor satellite imagery**
- 🗺️ **GIS and geological datasets**
- 🧪 **Geochemical information**
- 📊 **Geostatistical analysis**
- 🤖 **Machine Learning and Geospatial AI**
- 🪨 **Economic and exploration geology**

to identify **hydrothermal alteration zones**, map **lithological units**, characterize mineral systems, and improve **mineral exploration targeting**.

I hold an **M.Sc. in Geoscience from Western Kentucky University** and an **M.Sc. in Mineral Exploration from Sahand University of Technology**.

My academic and professional experience includes **mineral exploration research, geological mapping, hyperspectral and multispectral image analysis, 3D geological modeling, geostatistics, and data-driven exploration workflows**.

I have contributed to research and exploration projects involving **Freeport-McMoRan**, **Western Kentucky University**, and mineral districts across the **United States and Iran**.
