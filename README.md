I am an exploration geologist and geoscience researcher specializing in remote sensing, GIS, critical minerals, and mineral prospectivity mapping.

My work combines multi-sensor satellite imagery, geological and geochemical datasets, geostatistical methods, and machine learning to identify alteration zones, map lithologies, and improve mineral exploration targeting.

I hold an M.Sc. in Geoscience from Western Kentucky University and an M.Sc. in Mineral Exploration from Sahand University of Technology. My academic and professional experience includes mineral exploration research, geological mapping, hyperspectral and multispectral image analysis, 3D geological modeling, and cross-disciplinary work with geologists, engineers, GIS specialists, and environmental scientists.

My experience includes research and exploration projects involving Freeport-McMoRan, Western Kentucky University, and mineral districts in the United States and Iran.
