I am an **Exploration Geologist and Geoscience Researcher** specializing in **remote sensing, GIS, critical minerals, rare earth elements, and mineral prospectivity mapping**.

My work combines **multi-sensor satellite imagery**, **geological and geochemical datasets**, **geostatistical methods**, and **machine learning** to:

- ⛏️ identify **mineral exploration targets**
- 📷 detect **hydrothermal alteration zones**
- 🗺️ map **lithologies and geological structures**
- 📊 analyze **geochemical anomalies**
- 🛰️ interpret **multispectral and hyperspectral imagery**
- 🤖 improve exploration through **Geospatial AI and Machine Learning**

I hold an **M.Sc. in Geoscience from Western Kentucky University** and an **M.Sc. in Mineral Exploration from Sahand University of Technology**.

My academic and professional experience includes **mineral exploration research, geological mapping, hyperspectral and multispectral image analysis, 3D geological modeling, geostatistics, mineral resource evaluation, and multidisciplinary exploration projects**.

My experience includes research and exploration projects involving **Freeport-McMoRan**, **Western Kentucky University**, and mineral districts across the **United States and Iran**.
